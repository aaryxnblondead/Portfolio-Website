e:T6ab,<p>The problem: Quantitative trading teams need scenario-based forecasts of NIFTY-50 constituents under specific macroeconomic conditions, not point predictions, but conditional distributions that capture tail risk.</p>
<p>The constraint: The model has to run on a 16GB GPU during backtesting windows of under two seconds per step, while still capturing cross-sectional dependencies across all fifty stocks.</p>
<p>What I built: A conditional diffusion model that takes macro features (inflation, PMI, crude prices) as the conditioning signal and generates plausible NIFTY-50 paths using a structured attention mechanism over cross-sectional correlations.</p>
<p>The architecture uses a U-Net backbone with cross-attention on the macro conditioning vector, trained on five years of daily NIFTY-50 data augmented with synthetic volatility surfaces to handle tail-event coverage.</p>
